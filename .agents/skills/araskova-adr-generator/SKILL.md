# Araskova ADR Generator Skill
Write ADRs in docs/adr/YYYY-MM-DD-title.md for all complex architectural changes.
Format:
- Title & Metadata (Status: ACCEPTED, Date, Deciders)
- Context & Problem Statement
- Decision Drivers
- Considered Options
- Decision Outcome & Rationale
- Pros and Cons
